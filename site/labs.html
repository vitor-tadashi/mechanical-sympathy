Latency labs

Three experiments you can run in a few seconds. Turn one setting on, and see which part of the tail goes away.

Everything runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. The costs are the orders of magnitude of the guides, so the results are illustrative: they show the shape of each effect, not your host.

1. Quiet the core

One thread spins on CPU 5 and handles 50,000 messages in one second. Everything else that wants the CPU gets in its way. Remove the sources one by one.

Settings

One second of CPU 5. Each mark is an interruption: wide blue are other tasks, thin red the tick, orange RCU, purple NIC interrupts, black SMIs.
