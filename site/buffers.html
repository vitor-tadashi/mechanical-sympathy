Buffer simulator

A burst arrives, a ring holds it, software drains the ring into a second buffer, and a reader empties that one. Change any of them and see where the packets are lost. The model is the one in Concept: network buffers, §3.

It runs in your browser, sends nothing anywhere, and gives the same answer as scripts/size-buffers. The result is illustrative: it shows the shape of a burst, and it is not a measurement of your host.
